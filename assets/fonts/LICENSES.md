# Bundled font licenses

Every face under `assets/fonts/` is redistributed with the app, so every face
is recorded here: its bundled files and byte lengths, the copyright string
embedded in the binary, the license it travels under, and where it came from.
Thirty-two families are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose text is
reproduced at the bottom of this file. Two are not — Bouazzi Maghribi is MIT
and Yellowtail is Apache-2.0 — and both license texts are kept under
`assets/fonts/licenses/`.

Where a binary carries no license string (Tangerine) or no copyright string
(Bouazzi Maghribi, whose name table names only its author), the entry says so
rather than inventing one, and the license is taken from the upstream release
named in `Source`.

Runtime-downloaded faces (the Google Fonts picker, see
`docs/editor-remote-fonts.md`) are never bundled, so they are not recorded
here. They are OFL-1.1 Google Fonts fetched on demand from the Fontsource
mirror of `github.com/google/fonts`; that license permits the app embedding one
in an exported SVG, and the face's own OFL notice remains the attribution when
a user does so.

## Other licenses

`assets/fonts/licenses/` holds the two non-OFL texts:

- `APACHE-2.0.txt` — Yellowtail (`yellowtail-latin-400.ttf`), Apache License
  2.0. The copyright notice is the one embedded in the binary: Copyright (c)
  2011 by Brian J. Bonislawsky DBA Astigmatic (AOETI).
- `MIT.txt` — Bouazzi Maghribi (`bouazzi-maghribi-400.ttf`), MIT. The binary
  names its author, Mohamed Gallah (`https://github.com/iyadh`), and points its
  license URL at `https://mit-license.org`; it carries no year or copyright
  line, so the template placeholder in the text is left as published.
